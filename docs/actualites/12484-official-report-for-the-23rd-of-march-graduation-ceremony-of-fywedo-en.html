OFFICIAL REPORT FOR THE 23RD OF MARCH GRADUATION CEREMONY OF FYWEDO ENTREPRENEURSHIP ACADEMY AND IMPACT WORKSHOP

Saturday March 23rd 2024 was a very huge success .
The F&F Young Women Entrepreneurship Development Program FYWEDO Entrepreneurship Academy Graduation (2nd badge ),it was also the induction of some young women entrepreneurs who joined the FYWEDO membership arm.
This same event featured a very high level IMPACT WORKSHOP with two powerful panel discussions.
This event was hosted under the organization F&F YOUNG WOMEN ENTREPRENEURSHIP DEVELOPMENT ORGANIZATION(FYWEDO) in partnership with some organizations and companies ,BRITISH COUNCIL in Cameroon being the main partner for this year’s edition .
This event was attended by some officials ;
-The Inspector General of the Ministry of Small and Medium Sized Enterprises who represented ,His Excellency the Minister
of small and medium sized enterprises social economy and handicraft
-The Head of monitoring and Entrepreneurship Unit in the Ministry of Women’s Empowerment and the family who represented ,Her Excellency the minister of Women’s Empowerment and the family .
-His Excellency the British High Commission specially represented by the British Council Country Lead
-The General Manager of MTN Enterprise Unit
-Officials from the French Development -Cooperation
